Frequently Asked Questions

▸ How do I know your gold is authentic?
All our gold products come with a certificate of authenticity. Bars and coins are sourced directly from LBMA-approved refiners. Each item includes assay certification and unique serial numbers.
▸ What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and Cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT). All transactions are processed through our PCI-compliant, SSL-secured payment gateway.
▸ Is my shipment insured?
Yes, every shipment is fully insured for its complete value. We use Brink's and Malca-Amit for secure logistics. All packages are discreetly packaged and require adult signature on delivery.
▸ How does the Virtual Vault work?
Your Virtual Vault allows you to store purchased gold securely in our insured facility. You can view your holdings, track value changes, and request physical delivery at any time. Storage is free for VIP members.
▸ What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Gold bullion returns are subject to market price adjustments. Contact our support team to initiate a return.
▸ How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order through the "Track Order" link in the menu or your account dashboard.
▸ What is KYC Verification and why do I need it?
KYC (Know Your Customer) is a regulatory requirement for precious metals transactions over $10,000. It helps us prevent fraud and comply with anti-money laundering laws. Verification is quick and secure.
▸ Do you offer custom jewelry or bulk orders?
Yes! We specialize in custom gold jewelry and bulk bullion orders. Contact our customer care team via the Contact Support form to discuss your requirements and receive a custom quote.
